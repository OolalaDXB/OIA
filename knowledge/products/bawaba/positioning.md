# BAWABA — Positioning & authorised language (canonical)

**Approved one-liner** (external-safe):

> BAWABA is a control and evidence plane for AI gateways and enterprise AI
> agents.

Authorised framing: overlay/sidecar that gives enterprises decision control
(ALLOW / DENY / REVIEW), auditability and provenance over AI gateway and
agent actions, with an optional embedded sovereign gateway. **Deployable in
the client's perimeter or as a managed deployment** (two offered modes —
honest maturity per `fact-bawaba-deployment-modes`; never "not a SaaS" as a
flat claim). Single external identity: **bawaba.systems** + this one-liner
(current convergence, future direction not frozen).

Forbidden language: any wording implying incorporation in a specific
jurisdiction, certifications not held, capabilities in production that are
still at specification stage. See `constraints.md` and
`../../policies/disclosure.yaml`.
