Find information on UK public sector procurements and contracts

Find a Tender is where public sector buyers publish notices about procurements opportunities and contracts.

Summarised Search New

These summaries consolidate all notices from a procurement in one place to give you a current picture of its status. Use Notice Search (above) to view individual published notices.

Opportunities and contracts search cover procurements published from February 2025, under the Procurement Act 2023. For procurements under other legal regimes, use the Notice Search.
